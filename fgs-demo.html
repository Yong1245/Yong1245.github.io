Build 0.1.4 (5) · Android 16 / API 36 emulators · 18 September 2026. Timer: Google Play installation. Scheduled alarm: locally installed release APK from the same source.

Local alarm audio playback

A user creates a one-minute timer in Sound mode and starts it. The app moves to the home screen. When the timer becomes due, AlarmRingingService starts a mediaPlayback foreground service. Its notification offers Dismiss and Snooze controls. Dismiss stops playback and the service.
