Goals&Timers Privacy Policy

Operator and privacy contact: 권용익

Email: thebbunone@gmail.com

App: Goals&Timers (com.yonyu.goaltime)

Effective date: September 18, 2026

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores goal titles and descriptions, success and failure records, recorded time, timer and alarm settings, dream notes and display settings on your device to provide its features. No account or login is required. The current version has no feature that automatically sends these records to the developer's server.

2. Backups and transfers

When you create a backup, you choose its destination in the system file picker. Depending on the provider you choose, the file may be stored in the cloud under that provider's privacy policy. Backup ZIP/JSON files contain records and notes. The app does not password-encrypt these files. Checksums detect file corruption; they do not hide the contents.

The app permits Android backup and device-to-device transfer. Data may be backed up or moved to another device according to your OS, device and account settings. Recovery backups created before a restore remain in the app's local storage.

3. Notifications and permissions

The app uses Android permissions to display notifications, schedule exact alarms, restore schedules after reboot, vibrate and play alerts. Internet and network-state permissions support advertising and Google Play connections. This app build excludes Android advertising ID and AdServices permissions; this does not remove all identifier processing by advertising services. You can manage permissions and battery restrictions in your device settings.

4. Advertising and optional ad removal

When advertising is available, Google Mobile Ads banners appear below the bottom navigation on Dreams and Calendar. Requests restrict personalization and use a general-audience content rating for all users. We do not collect names or dates of birth for advertising, or send goal titles, records or dream notes as ad targeting information.

Advertising services may process IP addresses, app interactions, diagnostics and identifiers for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Restricting personalization does not eliminate all data processing. Ad privacy options are provided in Settings when required.

An optional one-time Google Play purchase removes both banners. Goals, timers, alarms, calendar and dreams remain available without purchasing. Google Play handles payment; the app does not receive card numbers. To verify and restore ad removal, the app stores the signed purchase record supplied by Google Play, including its purchase token, product ID, package name and purchase state, and uses Play purchase-query and acknowledgement APIs. Purchase records are excluded from app-record backups. Purchases can be restored with the same Google Play account. Clearing app data removes the local copy; Google Play manages its order history separately under Google policies.

5. Support email

Writing a message in the app and choosing Send opens an external email app. If you complete sending there, the operator receives your sender email address, message and any attachments you choose, to respond and resolve the issue. The app does not automatically attach personal records or diagnostic logs. You can use the app's main features without sending a support request.

Support messages are received through Gmail. External email services apply their own policies to transmission and storage. Do not send passwords, signing keys or personal records that are unnecessary to resolve your request.

6. Retention and deletion

Support correspondence received by the operator is deleted within one year after the request is resolved. To request access, correction or deletion, contact thebbunone@gmail.com. Do not send identity documents that are unnecessary for your request.

On-device records remain according to your edits, deletions and the app's record-retention behavior. Deleting a timer leaves its past recorded time in the calendar. Recovery backups, exported files and Android backups may remain separately from the records shown in the app. Removing local app data does not automatically delete external backups or previously sent email. Manage those copies separately in the relevant service or device settings.

7. Intended audience

Goals&Timers is designed for teens aged 13 and older and adults, and is not directed at children. A name or date of birth is not required to use the app. There is no public posting or user-to-user chat. The intended audience is separate from the app content rating.

8. Protecting information

The app's on-device storage follows Android's per-app access restrictions. Use a screen lock, keep your operating system updated and store exported backups safely. Support email and attachments are handled only as needed to respond and resolve issues, using the email service's security features and access controls. The app does not guarantee encryption across every email transmission segment or encrypt exported backups.

9. This webpage

This page contains no added analytics, advertising, login or contact form. The hosting provider GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. This is separate from automatic transmission of app records to the developer.

10. Changes

This page and its effective date will be updated when data handling or services change. Please use the contact above for privacy questions.
